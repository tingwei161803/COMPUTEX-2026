Visitor FAQ

Dates, venues, registration and the programs you can join.

When and where is COMPUTEX 2026?expand_more
June 2–5, 2026 in Taipei, across four venues: Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center Hall 1 & 2 (TaiNEX 1 & 2), TWTC Hall 1, and the Taipei International Convention Center (TICC).
How do I register or buy a ticket?expand_more
Visitor pre-registration is available online via the official COMPUTEX registration site. Pre-registering ahead of the show is the fastest way to get your badge and skip on-site queues.
What are the three core themes?expand_more
AI & Computing, Robotics & Mobility, and Next-Gen Tech. Together they trace the AI stack from silicon and servers, through robots and smart mobility, out to 6G, edge, quantum, security and sustainability.
What's new at the show this year?expand_more
Two new highlight zones debut at TWTC Hall 1 — the Robotics Zone and the TechXperience Zone — showing how robotics and physical AI are expanding into healthcare, logistics, retail and daily life.
What is InnoVEX?expand_more
InnoVEX is COMPUTEX's dedicated startup pavilion at TaiNEX 2. It connects early-stage companies with investors through the Meet-a-VC program (800+ investors), plus global buyer networking events and startup pitch contests.
Is the COMPUTEX Forum free?expand_more
The COMPUTEX Keynote & Forum executive sessions (7F, TaiNEX 2) require paid registration. The main exhibition halls are accessed with a visitor badge.
How can I join NVIDIA GTC Taipei?expand_more
NVIDIA hosts GTC Taipei alongside COMPUTEX (keynote on June 1; sessions June 1–4 at TICC). Registration and the session catalog are on NVIDIA's GTC Taipei site.

About COMPUTEX

Asia's premier ICT and AI tradeshow, and a global meeting point for the technology industry.

Founded in 1981, COMPUTEX Taipei has grown into one of the world's largest and most influential information and communications technology (ICT) shows. It is co-organized by TAITRA (Taiwan External Trade Development Council) and TCA (Taipei Computer Association).

Why it matters

  • A platform where the global AI ecosystem — chipmakers, system builders, startups and buyers — meets in one place
  • Home to NVIDIA GTC Taipei and the InnoVEX startup pavilion under one show week
  • A bellwether for where AI computing, robotics and connectivity are heading next

By the numbers (2025)

The 2025 edition drew a record 86,521 visitors from 152 countries, with roughly 1,400 exhibitors filling about 4,800 booths across 80,000 m² — the momentum COMPUTEX 2026 builds on.
